What is NSFAS?

The National Student Financial Aid Scheme (NSFAS) provides financial assistance to eligible South African students at public universities and TVET colleges. If you come from a household earning less than R350,000 per year, you may qualify for NSFAS funding to cover tuition, accommodation, and living allowances.

How to apply: Visit the NSFAS website and create a myNSFAS account. You will need your ID number, parent or guardian's ID, and proof of household income.

Apply on NSFAS website
